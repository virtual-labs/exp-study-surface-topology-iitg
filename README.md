### About the Experiment

This experiment studies the surface topology of nanoparticles using a scanning electron microscope (SEM).
A narrow beam of primary electrons from a field emission source is focused by electronic lenses and scanned across the specimen surface in a raster fashion using deflection coils.
Secondary electrons emitted from each scanned point, whose angle and velocity relate to the surface structure, are captured by a detector and amplified into an electronic signal.
The signal is converted into a two-dimensional image revealing the topographical features of the sample, while backscattered electrons additionally provide compositional contrast.
